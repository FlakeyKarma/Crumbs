Appearance

Each reader picks their own theme from the header, and it's remembered in their browser rather than on their account — a kitchen tablet and a laptop can look nothing alike. This sets which one a browser starts on before anyone chooses. Light and dark follow the reader's device, or the clock if the device has no preference.

Backup and restore

A backup holds every row and every uploaded photo in one file — the database alone would restore recipes pointing at pictures that are no longer there. Sessions, permissions and content types are left out: they are rebuilt from the code, and including them is the usual reason a restore fails.

What to do with what is here now

A backup of the current state is written to the project directory before anything is touched, whichever option you pick. Replacing clears sessions too, so you will be signed out.

The same two things from a terminal, which is what a scheduled backup would use: manage.py backup --to /path/file.tar.gz and manage.py restore /path/file.tar.gz.

Accounts and the rest

Users, passwords and anything structural live in the Django admin. Database location, allowed hosts and the built-in themes are environment variables and crumbs/settings.py; see the README.
