Grandma's Skillet Cornbread

By flakeykarma · added 12 March 2026
Ingredients
1 ½ cups cornmealBob's Red Mill100%
½ cup flourKing Arthur33%
1 ¼ cups buttermilk83%
2 eggs
6 tbsp butter, melted
2 tbsp honey
1 tsp baking powder
½ tsp baking soda
1 tsp salt
Method
1Heat the oven to 425°F with the skillet inside.
15 min
2Whisk cornmeal, flour, baking powder, soda and salt.
2 min
3Whisk buttermilk, eggs and honey; fold into the dry mix.
3 min
4Swirl butter in the hot skillet, pour in the batter.
1 min
5Bake until golden and pulling from the edges.
22 min
6Rest, then cut into wedges.
10 min
Notes · 3

Lemon Olive Oil Cake
Damp, fragrant, and better on the second day. Serve with barely sweetened cream and whatever berries are around.
200 g all-purpose flourKing Arthur100%
150 g sugar75%
120 ml olive oilFrantoia60%
3 eggs
2 lemons, zest and juice
80 ml whole milk40%
1 ½ tsp baking powder
Add ingredient
1Whisk eggs and sugar until pale and thick.
4 min
2Stream in the oil, then the zest, juice and milk.
3 min
3Fold in flour and baking powder until just smooth.
2 min
4Bake in a lined 9-inch tin at 350°F.
45 min
5Cool in the tin, then turn out and dust with sugar.
30 min
