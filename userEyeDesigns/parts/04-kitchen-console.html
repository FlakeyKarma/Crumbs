Why

Built for a phone propped on the counter: dark ground, one amber accent, 56px controls that work with wet hands. Uses the data model directly: every step's timing becomes a timer, every ingredient's percentage becomes a scaling column.

Tradeoff

Reads as equipment, not a cookbook. Dark-only tone can feel severe for browsing on the couch, and the density asks for careful typography to stay legible.
