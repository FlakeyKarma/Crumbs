Direction 5

Retro Cookbook

Zilla Slab, Karla

Why

A 1970s community cookbook: mustard, olive and brick blocks on cream, a slab serif, dotted leaders between ingredient and amount, numbered circles for steps, and a "from the kitchen of" byline for the author. Instantly recognizable and warm without being precious.

Tradeoff

The novelty can wear over daily use, the three-color palette limits how much state you can encode, and the chunky type takes room that long ingredient lists need.
