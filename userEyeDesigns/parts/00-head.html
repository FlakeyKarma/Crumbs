Five ways Crumbs could look

Each direction shows the same three jobs, browsing the library, reading one recipe, and adding or editing a recipe, on a phone and on a desktop. The sample recipes reflect the current data model: ingredients carry a source and a percentage, steps carry a timing, and notes carry a date.
