Direction 2

Ledger

Hanken Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono for every number

Why

Brutally minimal: one paper tone, one ink, hairlines and type. Amounts, timings and percentages line up in a monospaced column, so a recipe reads like a well-kept ledger and the library scans in seconds. Trivial to theme.

Tradeoff

Can feel cold and administrative. Without photos or color there is little warmth, so the direction leans entirely on typographic discipline and the quality of the copy.
