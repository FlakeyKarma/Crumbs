Direction 1

Rolodex

Cormorant italic, Quicksand, Caveat for notes

Why

Carries the existing mockup forward. Every recipe is a ruled index card in a box, so the app feels like a family collection rather than a database. Notes live on the back of the card in handwriting.

Tradeoff

Skeuomorphic and low density. A card holds one recipe at a time, so the library is browsed by flipping rather than scanning, and long ingredient lists fight the card metaphor.
